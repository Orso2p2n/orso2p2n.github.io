This was a birthday gift for a friend of mine, a little Animal Crossing themed toy made in a week, in Godot. The goal was simply to create an interactive scene featuring my friend's Animal Crossing character.

The player has access to a small list of interactions:
- Clicking on the ground makes the character walk to that destination.
- Clicking on the character makes it do a wave animation, or a hug animation if the plushie is out.
- Clicking on the camera icon changes the camera angle.
- Clicking on the cat button takes out or puts away the cat plushie. 

All the assets and animations were made by me, but the idea was heavily inspired by [SpicyBeefu's](https://x.com/spicyBeefu/status/1871617083680731611) work.
